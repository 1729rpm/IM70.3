- Race cap logic that worked (27 Sep 2026): cap = 155 + km until km 10, then 165 flat, last km free. Sit 2 to 4 under until km 10. Km 10 sentence check: if a short sentence is impossible, drop all caps by 3. Finish rule: 170 with 3 km to go, 173 with 2, 177 with 1, none for the last 500 m.
- Above 170, time budget roughly halves per 5 bpm: fresh about 25 min at 175, 12 at 180, 5 at 185; halve those at the end of a half marathon.
- HR recovery is good: 26 to 34 bpm drop in 60 s after 2 min VO2 reps; 12 to 14 bpm in 30 s at run stops.

## Drift and heat
- Indoor rides lose 9 to 21% efficiency over 2 to 3 h without a fan (HR 134 to 153 at constant power).
- Outdoor runs: 5% decoupling in evening heat while under-hydrated (23 Sep), 2% in the cool morning race with water at every station (27 Sep). About 11 bpm drift at constant pace over 100 min.
- Cooler by 5 to 6 C is worth 10 to 15 s/km at the same HR (23 Sep vs 27 Sep).
- Water over head and neck at every aid station is the main cooling tool.

## Fuelling that works

- Aid stations cost time in the Bengaluru half (crowded, full stops). Carry a soft flask; walk 5 s, two cups, go.
