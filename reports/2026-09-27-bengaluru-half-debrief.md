# Bengaluru Marathon half, 27 Sep 2026: debrief

**Result:** 2:21:30 at 21.1 km (watch), avg HR 164, max 181, negative split 72:06 / 70:36, fastest km last (5:57). Target was 2:20 to 2:24. PR of 2:20 (Feb 2026 70.3 run leg) missed by about 90 s, on 8 km/week of running and 7 kg over February weight.

## Plan vs execution
Cap 155 + km until km 10, then 165 flat, last km free. Executed within 2 bpm throughout; km 10 to 16 at 165 give or take 1 for 45 minutes. Finish rule (170 / 173 / 177 / free) used from km 18.

## What the data showed
- 165 bpm is comfortably under the ceiling: 70 min at 164 to 167 without runaway, 17.5 min continuous at 165+, HR tracked pace in both directions.
- Drift about 11 bpm at 6:40 pace over 100 min, inside the 8 to 12 estimate. Decoupling 2%, versus 5% on the 23 Sep rehearsal in evening heat while under-hydrated.
- Cooler air (22 C vs about 28 C on the watch) gave 6:41/km at HR 165 versus 6:53 four days earlier.
- Cadence 166 average, never below 160.
- Wrist HR trace was clean (no jumps over 15 bpm). Strap not worn (uncomfortable, slides).

## What worked
- Stepped HR caps with a flat middle and a late release.
- Fuel: gel at 0:35, 1:10, 1:45; salt caps at 0:55, 1:30; small gel pre-start. No stomach issues.
- Visor, no cap, no headphones; water over head and neck at stations.
- Race-week eating at maintenance with a low-fibre, carb-heavy Saturday.

## What to change
- Aid stations: stopping fully in crowds cost time. Carry a 250 to 500 ml soft flask, walk 5 s, two cups, go.
- Cap could rise to 168 from km 14; the 5:57 last km says 1 to 2 minutes were left.
- Sort the HR device before Goa: strap tighter with electrode gel, or an optical arm band, tested on two bricks.

## Carry-forward for Goa (1 Nov 2026)
Run line 155 to 160 in Goa heat off the bike (the fresh, cool equivalent of 165). Bike HR under 150. Two salt caps an hour. Recovery: no running until 1 Oct, easy swim and spin only.
