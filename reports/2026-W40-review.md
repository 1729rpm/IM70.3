## Key sessions
**Saturday ride.** Set as 6 x 15 min at race effort; outdoors it became one steady out-and-back. 90 km passed at 2:49 of riding (3:05 with three stops), average HR 147. Goa's plan is 3:20 at an average under 150, never above 155. The average complied; the shape did not: HR 144 at 33.0 km/h for the first half, 153 at 30.1 km/h for the second, a quarter of the ride above 155. That rise is drift (heart rate climbing while output holds or falls), and Goa's heat will enlarge it.

**Run off it.** HR held 152 to 160, around the 155 to 160 race band, 25 s/km faster than the Goa target needs. The first kilometre was the fastest (6:59); the plan wants it held back.
