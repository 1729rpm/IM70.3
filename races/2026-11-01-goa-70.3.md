## Bike course and estimate (2026-10-05)
Course, from public sources (no official elevation profile found, so low to medium trust): about 525 m of climbing over 90 km (finishers.com listing). That is no more per km than Rajat's 3 Oct route (574 to 648 m over 101.7 km). The difference is shape. Race reports from 2022 and 2024 describe two 45 km out-and-back loops with a few long climbs each (Bambolim, the Zuari bridge, a long drag to the U-turn) and descents at 50 to 60 km/h. A 2026 listing describes three 30 km laps along the Mandovi causeway instead, so the layout needs confirming from the athlete guide. The 3 Oct route was gentle rollers: half of it within 1% of flat, 0.4 km steeper than 3%, biggest single climb 24 m.

Why shape matters: a climb costs time that its descent does not give back, and the cost grows fast with gradient. Modelled for Rajat against the same distance on flat road: 1 km at 1.5% up and down loses 6 s; 1.7 km at 3.5% (60 m) loses 85 s; the same 60 m at 5% loses 115 s.

Estimate: 3:10, range 3:05 to 3:20; up to 3:25 if the course is nearer 750 m of climbing or the climbs nearer 5%. Built from the 3 Oct ride (90 km in 2:49 of riding, strap HR 147, race bike, no drafting): plus 6 to 10 min for the course shape and three U-turns, plus 4 min for sea-level air (about 9% denser than Bangalore's), plus 6 min for heat and holding the HR cap (taken as 7% less power; 3% to 10% moves it 3 min either way). Confidence low to medium, because the Goa profile is reconstructed from descriptions, not measured.

Method, so it can be rerun on a real course file: speed by gradient from the 3 Oct ride fitted to the standard cycling power equation (rider plus bike 94 kg, rolling resistance 0.005, drag area, meaning how much air the rider pushes, 0.30 to 0.38 m2). That gives 156 to 188 W on the flat plus 11 to 24 W per 1% of gradient, capped at 1.2 times flat power; coasting above 45 km/h, braking at 55 km/h; air density 1.06 kg/m3 in Bangalore, 1.155 in Goa. The model replays the 3 Oct ride 2% slow (2:52 against 2:49). The Targets above stay as they are until the plan is written.
