## Bike course and estimate (2026-10-05)
Course, from the official route file (Strava route by IRONMAN Asia Admin, uploaded by Rajat; elevation is read off a map, so medium trust): two laps, 92.0 km as drawn, 519 m of climbing. That is the same climbing per km as Rajat's 3 Oct route (5.6 against 5.7 m per km). `2026-11-01-goa-bike-course.csv` holds the profile every 250 m.

Each lap: 13 km of flat road through Panjim along the Mandovi with a U-turn at km 6.9; the Bambolim climb at km 13 (1.2 km at 5.0%, steepest 200 m 6.8%, 60 m up); down to sea level, over the Zuari bridge near km 24, then a drag up to the second U-turn at km 27.8 (36 m); back the same way, climbing Bambolim from the south at km 37 (65 m over 4.5 km, the last 1.2 km at 3.7%); a 1 km descent at 5% into Panjim. Lap 2 repeats from km 42, with the climbs at km 55 and km 79. Four U-turns and seven sharp corners in all.

Against the 3 Oct route: Goa is flat for longer (65% of it within 1% of flat, against 51%) but 10% of it is steeper than 3% up or down, against 0.4%. A climb costs time that its descent does not give back, and the cost grows fast with gradient. Modelled for Rajat against the same distance on flat road: 1 km at 1.5% up and down loses 6 s; a 60 m climb at 3.5% loses 85 s; the same 60 m at 5% loses 115 s.

Estimate: 3:13 for the 92.0 km as drawn (3:09 if the course rides as 90.0 km), range 3:05 to 3:20. Built from the 3 Oct ride (90 km in 2:49 of riding, strap HR 147, race bike, no drafting): plus 5 to 9 min for the course shape, U-turns and corners, plus 4 min for sea-level air (about 9% denser than Bangalore's), plus 6 min for heat and holding the HR cap (taken as 7% less power; 3% to 10% moves it 3 to 4 min either way), plus 4 min if the course is 92 km. About 16 to 19 min of the ride is on climbs of 3% or more; Bambolim from Panjim takes about 5.7 min at about 13 km/h. Confidence medium: real course, but one calibration ride and power inferred from speed, not measured. Rajat rode 3 Oct on aero bars and will use them in Goa, so the calibration carries over as it is; that position points to the lower drag areas below (0.30 to 0.34), where the estimate is 3:13 to 3:15.

Method, so it can be rerun: speed by gradient from the 3 Oct ride fitted to the standard cycling power equation (rider plus bike 94 kg, rolling resistance 0.005, drag area, meaning how much air the rider pushes, 0.30 to 0.38 m2). That gives 156, 172 or 188 W on the flat (for drag area 0.30, 0.34, 0.38) plus 24, 17 or 11 W per 1% of gradient, capped at 1.2 times flat power; coasting above 45 km/h, braking at 55 km/h, 10 km/h through U-turns, 20 km/h through sharp corners; air density 1.06 kg/m3 in Bangalore, 1.155 in Goa. The map elevation drops to river level where the course crosses the Zuari bridge; the profile file bridges that dip. The model replays the 3 Oct ride 2% slow (2:52 against 2:49).
