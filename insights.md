## 2026-09-28

- **Consistency is the binding constraint, not fitness.** Aug to Sep 2026: 4.7 real hours/week, run completion 30%, swim 14%. Fitness curves moved with gaps (cadence-work HR 131 to 140 after the 10 day gap; VO2max estimate slide). High confidence.
- **TrainingPeaks overstates volume by about 60% in good weeks** because MyWhoosh rides upload twice. Any analysis or coaching decision built on TP totals is wrong until deduplicated. High confidence.
- **Run LTHR is 168 to 172; Garmin's 178 is a formula.** Evidence: HR flat at 163 to 167 for 70 min (27 Sep), runaway above 168 to 170 on the treadmill (20 Sep), bike threshold reps at 163 to 167 plus a 5 to 8 bpm run offset. High confidence.
- **165 bpm is sustainable for a full half marathon.** 17.5 min continuous at 165+, 70 min at 164 to 167, HR tracked pace both ways, 2% decoupling (27 Sep). High confidence.
- **HR-capped, stepped pacing works for Rajat.** Executed within 2 bpm for 21 km, negative split, fastest km last. Cap 155 + km to km 10, then flat 165, last km free. The ceiling can rise to 168 from km 14 next time. High confidence.
- **Drift is large indoors, modest outdoors.** 9 to 21% efficiency loss on indoor rides without a fan; 2 to 5% on outdoor runs. Fan for quality sessions; heat exposure only for planned acclimation (10 to 28 Oct before Goa). Medium-high confidence.
- **Hydration and salt change the second half.** 23 Sep (under-hydrated, evening heat): 5% decoupling, exhaustion at minute 80. 27 Sep (water every station, salt schedule): 2% decoupling, negative split. Medium confidence (conditions differed too).
- **Cooler air is worth 10 to 15 s/km at the same HR** (23 Sep 28 C on the watch vs 27 Sep 22 C). Medium confidence.
- **The engine is bigger than the running shows.** Resting HR mid-40s, Garmin VO2max 46 to 50 (normally a 2:00 to 2:05 half), actual 2:21. The gap is weight (7 kg over usual), run economy at easy pace (cadence 155 to 162, ground contact 300 ms) and low run volume. Expect fast early gains (to about 2:10 from weight alone) then slower ones. Medium-high confidence.
- **Most of the VO2max-estimate drop is arithmetic from weight.** 49.6 x 76/83 = 45.4 vs 45.8 observed. Medium confidence (training decline also contributes).
- **A 70.3 run is 10 to 12% slower than his open half in cool conditions** (Feb 2026 ratio), plus 5 to 8% for Goa heat. Medium confidence, one data point.
- **Wrist HR is not usable for cap-based pacing** (jumps of 18 bpm in 20 s, one rep 25 bpm low), though the 27 Sep wrist trace was clean. Strap or optical arm band needed for Goa. High confidence.
- **Time budget above threshold roughly halves per 5 bpm** (25 min at 175, 12 at 180, 5 at 185 fresh; halve at the end of a half). Derived from 19 Aug reps, 20 Sep treadmill runaway, and standard 5K-to-threshold HR gaps. Low-medium confidence, wrist data at the top end.
- **1:45 open half is an 18 to 24 month project**; sub 2:00 by Feb or Mar 2027 is likely if weight returns to 76 and run frequency reaches 4 to 5 a week. Medium confidence.

## 2026-09-28, year review (reports/2026-year-review.md)

- **Fitness follows consistency with a four to six week lag.** Best curves in Jan 2026 after the Oct to Jan block; worst in Apr after the 38-day gap; Jul recovered to Jan run values; Aug to Sep slipped after two 7 to 11 day gaps. Medium confidence.
- **The 38-day stop after the Feb 70.3 cost about 20% of run pace at fixed HR and about 45% of bike power at fixed HR**, and the run took 12 to 14 interrupted weeks to recover; the bike had not recovered by Sep (heat is part of that). Medium confidence.
- **Skips are concentrated by day, not by session length.** Monday runs 23%, Sunday swims 4%, Friday swims 32%, Saturday bikes 41%; Tue to Thu 60 to 70%. Long sessions are completed as often as short ones. High confidence.
- **Low weeks follow low weeks, not spikes.** Week before a sub-40% week averages 51% completion and 30% less load than average; only one gap (Apr) followed a load spike. High confidence on the pattern.
- **Bike drift is Rajat's, not only the room.** 20% decoupling in the Gurgaon AC room in Oct 2025, 10% in Jan, 11 to 18% in Bangalore. Modest heat acclimation over the summer (18.5% Apr to 10.6% Sep). Medium confidence.
- **The Dec to Feb intensity mix (55% easy, 41% steady, 5% hard at 8 to 10 h/wk) produced the best curves; the 2026 Bangalore mix (about 50/35/13, then 39/37/24 in Sep, at 4 to 5 h/wk) produced the worst.** Medium confidence.
- **Off the bike Rajat runs faster, hotter and fades more** (outdoor bricks: HR 153, 6:48/km, 8.4% decoupling vs standalone HR 148, 7:30/km, 2%). Medium confidence, 8 bricks.
- **Run mechanics improved over the year while HR at fixed pace worsened**: cadence 164 to 170, contact 282 to 274 ms at 6:30 to 7:00/km; HR at that pace up 10 to 13 bpm since Jul. The loss is metabolic (weight, heat, volume), not form. Medium confidence.
- **Wrist maxima of 197 and 199 look real**, gradual rise, minutes above 185 at 5:04 to 5:35/km. Max HR about 200 stands. Medium confidence.
- **Resting HR rose from 47 to 51 (ten months) to 56.5 in Aug and 62 in Sep 2026**; Rajat attributes it to alcohol. Largest wellness change of the year; track weekly. Medium confidence on size, low on cause.
